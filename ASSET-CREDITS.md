# Original automatic rangefinder assets

automatic-lrf.obj and automatic-lrf-icon.png are original procedural assets created for this mod by Nero, with AI assistance. They are distributed under the repository MIT license.

The housing is generated from six independent analytic cuboids. The icon is an orthographic drawing of this new mesh with an optical pane matching the runtime glass position. Generate-OriginalRangefinder.py is the reproducible source. No third-party model, converted mesh, screenshot, tracing or adapted topology is used in these assets.
